Welded Wire Mesh

Welded Wire Mesh & Cage Wire

Rigid spot-welded wire mesh in custom mesh spacing and panel sizes, made from bright mild steel, hot-dip galvanized and stainless (Cr-Ni) wire.

  • Wire Diameter: Ø 2.00 - 8.00 mm
  • Mesh Spacing: 20x15 mm - 100x50 mm

Wire Mesh Cable Trays

Wire Mesh Cable Trays & Fittings

High-ventilation wire tray systems for data centers, industrial plants and automation lines; dust does not accumulate and cables run cooler.

  • Height (H): 35 mm / 55 mm / 105 mm
  • Wire Diameter: 4 / 4.5 / 5 / 6 / 7.5 mm
  • Width (W): 50 mm - 900 mm
  • Finish: Pre-galvanized, Stainless SS304 / SS316
